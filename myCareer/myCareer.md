For example, a wearable device should be able to resolve the QRS complex of an ECG signal that can flag potential arrhythmias, even though it is not expected to match the medical-grade clarity of a standard 12-lead ECG.

- Thesis: *A High Input Impedance Chopper Stabilized Amplifier for Interfacing Dry Electrodes* 

  - Proposed an input impedance (Zin) boosting architecture optimized for bio-potential signal acquisition with dry electrodes, featuring an intelligent capacitor reconfiguration strategy within the signal chain.   
  - The crux of this work is that, it breaks the fundamental charge-discharge cycle(a well known phenomenon) in switch capacitor circuits that degrades the input impedance by making it completely resistive. Instead, it proves that a unique switching pattern among the differential capacitors of the input network, can provide a pure capacitive impedance - thus improving the input impedance significantly. 

- A part of my MS work is in [preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13129)
